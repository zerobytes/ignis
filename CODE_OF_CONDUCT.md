# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Critique the work with evidence;
welcome questions and people with different backgrounds and experience.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, publishing someone's
private information and repeated unwanted contact are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive threads and
restrict participation. Decisions should be proportionate to the behavior.
Use GitHub's private report controls for abusive content; do not publish
private information in an issue. Security vulnerabilities follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests and discussions.
