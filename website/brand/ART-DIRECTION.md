# Art briefs

## Identity board

Create a refined identity for IGNIS, a 3D game engine: the spark of an idea becoming a playable world. A large standalone mark and the same mark beside the word IGNIS on warm charcoal. A sculptural vermilion ember rising into an asymmetrical flame, with a pointed portal cut through the center. Compact, legible silhouette, few curves, architectural uppercase lettering with diagonal terminals. Warm ivory letters. No controllers, esports imagery, circular badge, shadows or glow.

## Standalone mark

Only the flame and portal symbol. Preserve the ascending curved flame, smaller right-hand tip and pointed doorway in negative space. Vermilion fill; transparent background and interior. No wordmark or backdrop.

## Horizontal signature

The same flame/portal at left, ivory IGNIS at right. Match the architectural lettering of the board. Clean solid letter strokes and smooth contours. Dark charcoal background, no supporting copy. The signature is intended for dark surfaces.

## Portal atmosphere

A monumental abstract doorway formed from dark basalt, shaped like an asymmetrical sculpted flame, standing on a volcanic landscape. A narrow molten-orange interior lights the portal and the cracks at its base. Fine eroded, stratified stone; restrained embers and a few hovering obsidian fragments, suggesting a world assembling. Low viewpoint, quiet dramatic composition, physically plausible materials, near-black stone and warm horizon. No people, text, buildings or purple/blue neon. Conceptual brand artwork, explicitly separate from engine screenshots.
